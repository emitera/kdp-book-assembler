# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 2026

At Birdy Pages (operated by emITera, Haifa, Israel), we understand that as an author or illustrator, the privacy and security of your unpublished work are your highest priorities. 

## 1. How We Process Your Files (Zero-Upload Guarantee)
Birdy Pages is built on a **client-side architecture**. This means that when you drag and drop your cover images and interior pages into our tool, **they never leave your computer**. 
* The PDF generation, image resizing, and transparency flattening happen entirely inside your web browser. 
* We do not upload, transmit, or store your manuscript or cover files on our servers. 

## 2. Information We Do Collect
To provide our Service, manage subscriptions, and offer customer support, we collect the following limited information:
* **Account Information:** Your email address when you register (via Email or Google Auth).
* **Billing Information:** We do not store your credit card details. All payment processing is handled by secure third-party providers (Allpay, PayPal). We only store your subscription status and billing history.
* **Support Data:** If you contact us via our Support form, we collect your name, email, and the contents of your message.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use your email address solely to:
* Authenticate your access to the Pro features.
* Send you important transactional emails (e.g., password resets, payment receipts, subscription expiration reminders).
* Respond to your support inquiries.

## 4. Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is only shared with essential service providers (such as Supabase for authentication and Allpay/PayPal for billing) strictly for the purpose of operating the Service.

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions or concerns about your privacy, please contact us at **support@birdypages.io**.
