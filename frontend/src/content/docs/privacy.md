# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 2026

At Birdy Pages (operated by emITera, Haifa, Israel), we understand that as an author or illustrator, the privacy and security of your unpublished work are your highest priorities. 

## 1. How We Process Your Files (Zero-Upload Guarantee)
Birdy Pages is built on a **client-side architecture**. This means that when you drag and drop your cover images and interior pages into our tool, **they never leave your computer**. 
* The PDF generation, image resizing, and transparency flattening happen entirely inside your web browser or local desktop app. 
* We do not upload, transmit, or store your manuscript or cover files on our servers. 

## 2. Information We Do Collect
To provide our Service, manage subscriptions, and offer customer support, we collect the following limited information:
* **Account Information:** Your email address when you register (via Email or Google Auth).
* **Billing and Payment Information:** **We do NOT store or process your full credit card numbers, expiration dates, or CVV security codes on our servers.** All payment transactions are handled directly, securely, and seamlessly by our certified Merchant of Record partner (**Paddle.com**). Paddle complies with strict Payment Card Industry Data Security Standards (**PCI-DSS Level 1**). We only receive and retain confirmation tokens, transaction identifiers, license keys, subscription status, and billing history.
* **Support Data:** If you contact us via our Support form, we collect your name, email, and the contents of your message.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use your email address solely to:
* Authenticate your access to Pro and Desktop features.
* Deliver your software license keys and transaction receipts.
* Send you important transactional emails (e.g., password resets, payment confirmations, subscription renewal notices).
* Respond to your support inquiries.

## 4. Data Sharing with Third Parties
We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is shared strictly with essential service providers for the purpose of operating the Service:
* **Authentication and Database:** Supabase (for secure user authentication and database management).
* **Merchant of Record & Payment Processing:** **Paddle.com** (to securely facilitate payment authorization, subscription billing, fraud prevention, and global tax compliance). When you complete a transaction, Paddle processes your payment data in accordance with Paddle's Privacy Policy.
* **Transactional Email Delivery:** Resend API (for license key delivery and support notifications).

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions or concerns about your privacy, please contact us at **support@birdypages.io**.
