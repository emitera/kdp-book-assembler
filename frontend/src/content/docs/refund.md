# Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Last Updated:** July 2026

Birdy Pages (operated by emITera) strives to provide a flawless experience for KDP authors. Because our Service provides digital goods and immediate access to premium features, our refund policy is structured as follows, in accordance with applicable consumer protection laws.

## 1. Subscriptions (Monthly and Yearly Pro)
* **Cancellations:** You may cancel your Monthly or Yearly subscription at any time. Once canceled, you will continue to have access to the Pro features until the end of your current paid billing cycle.
* **Refunds:** We do not offer prorated refunds for unused days or months of a subscription. 

## 2. One-Time Pass (Single Project)
Because the One-Time Pass allows immediate generation and downloading of a complete, unwatermarked PDF, **all sales are final and non-refundable**, with one strict exception:

**Technical Failure Exception:**
If the PDF file generated by Birdy Pages is rejected by the Amazon KDP platform strictly due to formatting errors caused by our software (e.g., incorrect spine calculations, bleed errors, or transparency flattening failures), you are eligible for a resolution. 

To claim this:
1. Contact us at **support@birdypages.io** within 14 days of purchase.
2. Provide screenshots of the specific error message provided by the Amazon KDP previewer.
3. Upon verification, you may choose between a **Full Refund** of the One-Time Pass fee or a **Free Generation Credit** once our support team resolves the technical issue.

## 3. Billing Descriptor
Please be aware that all purchases are processed by our parent legal entity. The charge on your bank or credit card statement will appear as **Birdy Pages** or **emITera**. We do not issue refunds for "unrecognized charges" if they originate from emITera or Birdy Pages for the use of our Service.
