Because our Service provides digital goods and immediate access to premium features, our refund policy is structured as follows, in accordance with applicable consumer protection laws and Paddle's Merchant of Record framework.

## 1. Subscriptions (Monthly and Yearly Pro)
* **Cancellations:** You may cancel your Monthly or Yearly subscription at any time. Once canceled, you will continue to have access to the Pro features until the end of your current paid billing cycle.
* **Refunds:** We do not offer prorated refunds for unused days or months of a subscription. 

## 2. One-Time Pass & Desktop Licenses
Because the One-Time Pass and Desktop License provide immediate software activation and unwatermarked generation capabilities, **all sales are final and non-refundable**, with one strict exception:

**Technical Failure Exception (Refund Policy Guarantee):**
If the PDF file generated by Birdy Pages is rejected by the Amazon KDP platform strictly due to formatting errors caused by our software (e.g., incorrect spine calculations, bleed errors, or transparency flattening failures), you are eligible for a resolution. 

To claim this:
1. Contact us at **support@birdypages.io** within 14 days of purchase.
2. Provide screenshots of the specific error message provided by the Amazon KDP previewer.
3. Upon verification, you may choose between a **Full Refund** of the purchase fee or **Free Generation Credits / Replacement License** once our support team resolves the technical issue.

## 3. Refund Processing via Paddle (Merchant of Record)
All refund requests and cancellations are processed through **Paddle**, our authorized Merchant of Record:

* For orders completed through Birdy Pages, refund requests can be submitted by contacting our support team at **support@birdypages.io** or through Paddle's buyer support portal.
* As the Merchant of Record, transaction verification, financial processing, and return of funds directly to your original payment method are handled securely through Paddle's infrastructure.

## 4. Billing Descriptor
Charges will appear on your bank or credit card statement as:
* **PADDLE.NET * BIRDYPAGES** or **PADDLE.NET**.

We do not issue refunds for "unrecognized charges" if they correspond to an active subscription or verified license purchase on Birdy Pages.
